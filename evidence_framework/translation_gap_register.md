# Translation Gap Register

## 1. Purpose

This register identifies the gap between currently available scientific evidence and the information that would be required for responsible translational research.

The selected demonstration concept is:

**Curcuma longa L. (turmeric/curcumin) — evidence mapping related to knee osteoarthritis.**

This register does not establish efficacy, safety, manufacturing suitability, regulatory approval or commercial viability.

Its purpose is to identify:

- What is known
- What remains uncertain
- What requires further research
- What would require formulation work
- What would require safety validation
- What would require regulatory assessment

---

# 2. Translation Principle

The framework follows:

Traditional knowledge
→
Research hypothesis
→
Preclinical evidence
→
Human evidence
→
Translation assessment
→
Future R&D questions

The presence of evidence at one level does not automatically establish the next level.

For example:

Traditional use ≠ clinical efficacy

In-vitro activity ≠ human effectiveness

Animal evidence ≠ human effectiveness

Human study evidence ≠ universal effectiveness

Scientific evidence ≠ automatic product approval

---

# 3. Translation Status Categories

Each gap is assigned one of the following categories:

- KNOWN
- UNCERTAIN
- FURTHER_R&D
- FORMULATION_WORK
- SAFETY_VALIDATION
- REGULATORY_ASSESSMENT
- UNKNOWN

---

# 4. Known Evidence

## TG-001 — Historical/traditional context

**Status:** KNOWN

Public sources document historical and traditional use of turmeric.

**Evidence basis:**

- SRC-001
- EV-001
- CLM-001

**Boundary:**

Historical use establishes historical context only.

It does not establish clinical efficacy, safety, dose or regulatory approval.

---

## TG-002 — Human scientific evidence exists

**Status:** KNOWN

Peer-reviewed systematic reviews and meta-analyses have evaluated turmeric/curcumin interventions in arthritis and osteoarthritis-related research.

**Evidence basis:**

- SRC-002
- SRC-003
- SRC-004
- SRC-005
- SRC-006

**Boundary:**

The existence of human evidence does not mean that every turmeric or curcumin preparation has the same effect.

---

## TG-003 — Evidence-quality methodology exists

**Status:** KNOWN

Established evidence-certainty methodologies consider factors such as risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness and imprecision.

**Evidence basis:**

- SRC-007
- EV-007
- CLM-007

---

# 5. Uncertain Evidence

## TG-004 — Generalizability across preparations

**Status:** UNCERTAIN

Different studies may use different turmeric or curcumin preparations.

The available evidence does not automatically establish that findings from one preparation apply to every other preparation.

**Why uncertain:**

- Different formulations may be used.
- Extract characteristics may differ.
- Study interventions may not be equivalent.
- Bioavailability characteristics may differ.

**Required action:**

Further evidence characterization and product-specific assessment would be required.

---

## TG-005 — Generalizability across populations

**Status:** UNCERTAIN

Evidence from selected study populations cannot automatically be generalized to every population.

**Why uncertain:**

Study populations, inclusion criteria, age ranges, disease characteristics and other factors may differ.

**Required action:**

Population-specific evidence assessment would be required.

---

## TG-006 — Long-term interpretation

**Status:** UNKNOWN

The available evidence map does not establish the long-term effects of every possible turmeric/curcumin preparation.

**Required action:**

Do not infer long-term outcomes from short-term evidence.

---

# 6. Further R&D Questions

## TG-007 — Mechanistic clarification

**Status:** FURTHER_R&D

Further research may be required to clarify biological mechanisms relevant to specific research questions.

Mechanistic findings should not automatically be converted into clinical-effectiveness claims.

---

## TG-008 — Evidence consistency

**Status:** FURTHER_R&D

Further research may be required where study findings differ in magnitude, direction or certainty.

**Important principle:**

Contradictions must remain visible until there is sufficient evidence to explain them.

---

## TG-009 — Reproducibility

**Status:** FURTHER_R&D

Independent replication may be needed to determine whether reported findings are reproducible across appropriate research settings.

---

# 7. Formulation Work

## TG-010 — Preparation/formulation comparability

**Status:** FORMULATION_WORK

Research evidence may involve specific extracts, preparations or formulations.

A future product-development programme would need to establish whether its formulation is comparable to the intervention studied in the literature.

**Current status:**

UNKNOWN.

No formulation equivalence is assumed.

---

## TG-011 — Product-specific characteristics

**Status:** FORMULATION_WORK

A hypothetical future product would require characterization of relevant product attributes before literature findings could be meaningfully mapped to that product.

**Current status:**

UNKNOWN.

This framework does not perform formulation development.

---

# 8. Safety Validation

## TG-012 — Product-specific safety

**Status:** SAFETY_VALIDATION

Published literature may provide safety information for particular study populations and interventions.

It does not automatically establish the safety of every future preparation or product.

**Current status:**

UNKNOWN.

Appropriate safety assessment would be required for any future development programme.

---

## TG-013 — Interaction and population-specific safety questions

**Status:** SAFETY_VALIDATION

Potential safety questions may depend on:

- Population
- Exposure
- Preparation
- Duration
- Concomitant medicines
- Existing conditions

These questions cannot be resolved by the current evidence map alone.

---

# 9. Regulatory Assessment

## TG-014 — Product regulatory classification

**Status:** REGULATORY_ASSESSMENT

The regulatory pathway for a future product depends on its intended use, formulation, jurisdiction and product category.

This evidence framework does not determine regulatory status.

**Current status:**

UNKNOWN.

---

## TG-015 — Regulatory approval

**Status:** UNKNOWN

No claim of regulatory approval is made by this project.

Regulatory status must be independently verified for a specific product and jurisdiction.

# 11. Translation Decision Rules

The following rules apply:

### Rule 1

Do not translate traditional knowledge directly into efficacy claims.

### Rule 2

Do not translate in-vitro findings directly into clinical claims.

### Rule 3

Do not translate animal findings directly into human effectiveness.

### Rule 4

Do not generalize one formulation to every formulation.

### Rule 5

Do not hide contradictory findings.

### Rule 6

Do not fill missing evidence with assumptions.

### Rule 7

Use `UNKNOWN` when evidence is insufficient.

### Rule 8

Separate scientific evidence from formulation development.

### Rule 9

Separate scientific evidence from regulatory assessment.

### Rule 10

Record the source and evidence identifiers for every important conclusion.
