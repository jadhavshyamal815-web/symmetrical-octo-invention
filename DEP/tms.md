## Role

TMS provides strategy alignment for the Scientific Evidence and Translational Research Mapping task.

TMS does not independently validate scientific claims, approve formulations, or replace scientific evidence review.

## Task Context

This Test Task 2 builds a reusable evidence-to-translation research framework for a non-sensitive public-domain botanical concept.

The framework is designed to support disciplined movement from:

Public source
→ evidence classification
→ claim traceability
→ contradiction handling
→ translation-gap identification
→ future R&D decision input

## Strategic Alignment

The work supports a reusable scientific evidence infrastructure that can be applied to additional non-sensitive research concepts.

The framework prioritizes:

- Evidence provenance
- Source traceability
- Evidence-quality classification
- Explicit uncertainty
- Contradiction visibility
- Reproducibility
- Separation of evidence from product claims
