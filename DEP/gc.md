# GC — Governance and Authority Boundary

## Role

GC is relevant only where the research workflow creates authority, governance, compliance or decision claims.

This Test Task 2 does not grant Shyamal authority to validate, approve or authorize a formulation or product.

## Current Applicability

GC review is currently limited because this task is an evidence-mapping and research-infrastructure exercise.

The framework does not make:

- Regulatory approval claims
- Product approval claims
- Clinical authorization claims
- Manufacturing authorization claims
- Safety approval claims
- Commercial authorization claims

## Scientific Evidence Boundary

Scientific evidence classification is not equivalent to regulatory or organizational approval.

The framework may identify:

- Evidence strength
- Evidence limitations
- Contradictions
- Unknowns
- Translation gaps

It does not convert these findings into an authorization decision.

## Authority Boundary

Shyamal's role in this task is limited to:

- Evidence collection
- Evidence classification
- Literature analysis
- Source traceability
- Contradiction documentation
- Translation-gap mapping
- Reusable framework development

Shyamal does not independently approve:

- Formulations
- Therapeutic claims
- Safety conclusions
- Regulatory submissions
- Manufacturing processes
- Commercial claims

## When GC Consultation Would Be Required

GC or appropriate governance review would become relevant if future work creates:

1. A formal authority or approval statement.
2. A regulatory interpretation.
3. A compliance determination.
4. A product or therapeutic claim requiring governance review.
5. A decision that depends on legal or regulatory authority.

Such decisions must be reviewed by the appropriate authorized function rather than inferred from this research framework.

## Principle

Evidence infrastructure informs responsible research decisions.

It does not itself constitute scientific, regulatory, legal, manufacturing or commercial authorization.
